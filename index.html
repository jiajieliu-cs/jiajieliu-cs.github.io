About

I am an incoming Ph.D. student at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (PolyU), where I will be advised by Dr. Chenhang He and Prof. Lei Zhang. Prior to that, I received my M.S. degree from Peking University (PKU) under the supervision of Prof. Mengyuan Liu. I received my bachelor’s degree from Northwestern Polytechnical University (NWPU) in 2023.
